Background

I study the neural underpinnings of visual memory and visual attention. Currently, I am investigating how neural connectivity shapes cognition as a postdoctoral researcher in dr. Eelke Spaak's Brain and Experience lab. For my latest projects, please check out the Research tab on this website or my Google Scholar page. In 2026, I obtained my Ph.D. (Cum Laude) from Utrecht University supervised by Stefan Van der Stigchel, Marnix Naber, and Christoph Strauch.
